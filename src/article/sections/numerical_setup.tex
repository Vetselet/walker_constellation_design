\subsection{Spatial and temporal discretization}
\label{subsec:coverage_discretization}

Coverage constraint in~\eqref{eq:optimization_problem} is continuous in both space and time. 
Its direct numerical verification would require evaluating coverage asessment for every point 
$Q\in\mathcal{R}$ and for every instant $t\in\mathcal{T}$. Since both $\mathcal{R}$ and $\mathcal{T}$
are continuous sets, the verification involves an uncountable set of states and cannot be 
performed directly within a numerical optimization procedure. Therefore, continuous coverage constraint
is replaced by a finite numerical approximation obtained by discretizing both spatial and temporal domains.

\paragraph{Spatial discretization}

The continuous region $\mathcal{R}$ is replaced by an H3 grid of a prescribed resolution $\rho$ \cite{h3docs}. 
H3 provides a hierarchical, hexagonal discretization of the terrestrial surface and belongs to the broader class 
of discrete global grid systems (DGGS) \cite{sahr2003dggs,ogc_dggs}. Hexagonal spatial representations, including H3-based maps, 
are also used in telecommunication connectivity analysis \cite{itu_e813}.
Let
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{Q}_{\rho}=\{Q_1,\ldots,Q_{N_{\rho}}\}
    \label{eq:h3_point_set}
\end{equation}
denote the set of centers of the H3 cells of resolution $\rho$ selected for the region of interest. 
The spatial resolution is chosen with respect to the characteristics of the payload coverage pattern. 
For the discrete coverage test, the indicator~\eqref{eq:coverage_indicator} is averaged over the selected points:
\begin{equation}
    \widehat{\mu}_{\rho}(t,\mathbf{x})=
    \frac{1}{N_{\rho}}\sum_{j=1}^{N_{\rho}}
        \chi(Q_j,t,\mathbf{x}).
    \label{eq:discrete_coverage_metric}
\end{equation}
This quantity represents the fraction of covered test points. If an approximation of the area fraction 
in~\eqref{eq:coverage_metric} is required, the individual contributions should additionally be weighted 
by the areas of the corresponding parts of $\mathcal{R}$. Such weights do not affect whether all selected
test points are covered. Thus, $\widehat{\mu}_{\rho}(t,\mathbf{x})=1$ means that the centers of
all selected H3 cells are covered at timepoint $t$. Increasing $\rho$ improves the spatial resolution of the 
verification but also increases the computational cost.

\paragraph{Temporal discretization}

For the temporal verification, the continuous interval $\mathcal{T}$ is replaced by a finite grid:
\begin{equation}
    \begin{gathered}
        \mathcal{T}_M=\{t_0,t_1,\ldots,t_M\}\subset\mathcal{T},\\
        t_{\mathrm{start}}=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_M=t_{\mathrm{end}}.
    \end{gathered}
    \label{eq:time_grid}
\end{equation}
Here, $M\in\mathbb{N}$ is the number of temporal subintervals, so that $\mathcal{T}_M$ contains $M+1$ evaluation timepoints 
and includes both endpoints of $\mathcal{T}$.
The temporal spacing is selected with respect to the shortest characteristic time scale associated with a potentially 
critical change in coverage geometry. In the present problem, the relevant scales correspond to the motion 
over street of coverage half width, the along track separation between adjacent spacecraft within same orbital plane, 
and the orbital arc affected by the geoprotection constraint. Denoting the corresponding characteristic times by $\tau_{\mathrm{cov}}$, 
$\tau_{\mathrm{plane}}$, and $\tau_{\mathrm{gp}}$, respectively, define
\begin{equation}
    \tau_{\min}=\min\left\{\tau_{\mathrm{cov}},\tau_{\mathrm{plane}},\tau_{\mathrm{gp}}\right\}.
    \label{eq:min_characteristic_time}
\end{equation}
To ensure that each characteristic interval contains several evaluation points, the temporal grid is chosen such that
\begin{equation}
    \max_{k=0,\ldots,M-1}\left(t_{k+1}-t_k\right)\leq\frac{\tau_{\min}}{N_t},
    \label{eq:time_step_condition}
\end{equation}
where $N_t$ is a prescribed parameter of temporal resolution.
The coverage constraint in~\eqref{eq:optimization_problem} is then evaluated in the discrete form:
\begin{equation}
    \min_{t\in\mathcal{T}_M}
        \widehat{\mu}_{\rho}(t,\mathbf{x})=1.
    \label{eq:discrete_time_coverage}
\end{equation}
This condition requires all selected H3 cell centers to be covered at every timepoint of the temporal grid. 
Since it represents a sampled approximation of the original continuous constraint, coverage between adjacent 
spatial and temporal samples is not guaranteed analytically, and therefore requires additional check.
